A fan-made playable remix inspired by Nicky Case's public-domain game.
Explore, make choices, help your anxiety, and learn that fear doesn't have to drive.

WASD / Arrow keys to move • SPACE to comfort • E to interact

How to play

Move: WASD or arrow keys
Comfort pulse: SPACE — lowers fear around you
Interact: E — talk, open doors and activate story moments
Goal: collect Courage Sparks, find the exit, and keep your fear meter from filling.
